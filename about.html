Appalachian Roots • White-Glove Hospitality

Redefining The Holiday Evergreen Tradition

Founded under Xmas Made Inc. and the broader Global Citizen Joe, Inc. parent enterprise, Christmas Tree Joe unites generational North Carolina timber stewardship with full-service urban white-glove installation and zero-waste post-holiday recycling.

🌲

Appalachian Timber Stewardship

We partner directly with family plantations in Jefferson and Ashe County, North Carolina—the Fraser Fir capital of the world. By locking in multi-year pre-orders, we eliminate retail speculation risk for local timber growers and guarantee fair wholesale equity.

Grade #1 USDA Certified Evergreens
🚚

White-Glove Urban Logistics

Say goodbye to rooftop tying, dropping needles, and heavy lifting. Our Philadelphia metro dedicated fleet delivers fresh-cut trees to Center City, Main Line estates, and Tri-State residences, executing in-home stand placement, unbaling, hydration, and needle sweep.

Full In-Home Setup Included
♻️

100% Zero-Waste Circular Economy

When the holidays end, our service continues. Every collected tree in January is returned to regional facilities: needles are steam-distilled into therapeutic essential oils, hardwood trunks are split for cured firewood, and remaining wood fibers become garden mulch and compost.

0% Landfill Impact Guaranteed
