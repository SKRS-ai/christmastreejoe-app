🤝 Civic Partnership & Community Development

Rooted in Community,
Grown with Purpose

Partnering with Thin Black and Brown Lines USA (tbblusa.org) to bring seasonal warmth, neighborhood workforce training, youth creative arts, and circular urban greening to Philadelphia.

🎨

Youth Holiday Arts

TBBLUSA coordinates holiday ornament workshops where Philadelphia youth design handcrafted timber decorations for Center City public park displays.

☕

Winter Warmth Hubs

Hosting seasonal community stations offering complimentary hot cider, winter resource packs, and neighborhood outreach in Center City parks.

🚛

Workforce Training

Creating paid seasonal employment pathways in green logistics, inventory staging, and white-glove living room installation for local workers.

🌱

Urban Garden Mulch

100% of trees picked up in January are converted into organic mulch donated to Philadelphia community gardens and urban agriculture hubs.

Transparent Operational Alliance

How Our Public-Private Partnership Works

A seamless operational balance between corporate infrastructure and non-profit community engagement.

🏛️

Thin Black and Brown Lines USA

501(c)(3) Civic & Community Partner
  • Public park festive staging, tree decorations, and lighting aesthetics.
  • Youth, family, and community greeting ambassadors at public displays.
  • Distribution of civic educational materials and winter support kits.
  • Direct beneficiary of community donations and park beautification funds.
🏢

Global Citizen Joe, LLC. / Xmas Made Inc.

Enterprise Operations & Fiscal Sponsor
  • Holds commercial business licensing, permits, and $1M+ liability insurance.
  • Direct mountain grower procurement and multi-depot timber logistics.
  • White-glove residential doorstep dispatch and hydration setup.
  • 100% zero-waste January timber pickup, chipping, and circular processing.
