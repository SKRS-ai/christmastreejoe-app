Our Community Pillars

How We Reinvest in Philadelphia & Regional Communities

🌱

Urban Garden Mulch Grants

100% of January collected trees are converted into nutrient-dense, chemical-free organic mulch. We donate bulk deliveries directly to West Philly, North Philly, and Camden community food hubs to enrich topsoil and retain spring moisture.

Zero Municipal Waste
🚚

Youth Green-Collar Training

In partnership with TBBLUSA, we sponsor seasonal apprenticeships for young adults in logistics management, GPS telemetry dispatch, warehouse cross-docking, and commercial arborist handling.

Living-Wage Stipends
🚜

Independent Grower Parity

We bypass commercial broker cartels by issuing guaranteed pre-season purchase orders to multi-generational family and minority-owned timber growers across Ashe, Watauga, and Grayson counties.

Fixed Harvest Contracts
🌍

1% Stripe Climate Removal

Every transaction commits an automatic 1% to frontier scientific permanent carbon removal technologies, ensuring our circular economy actively cools the atmosphere for future generations.

Permanent Mineralization
