🏛️ The Presidential Choice

Fraser Firs have been selected for the White House Blue Room more than any other evergreen species in history.

✨ "Silver Lining" Needles

Underneath every needle are two silvery-white stomatal bands, giving the tree a natural shimmering halo effect.

📐 Tiered Architecture

Noble Firs grow in rigid, evenly spaced horizontal tiers ("whorls") that easily support heavy heirloom glass.

💧 Rapid Hydration

In the first 48 hours indoors, fresh-cut evergreens can drink up to 1 gallon of water per day to preserve needle suppleness.
