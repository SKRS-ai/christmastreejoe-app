One-Click Mobile App Installation

No app store required. Install directly to your device for offline support, fast loading, and delivery push notifications.

🍏

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

iOS Installation Guide

  1. Open Safari and navigate to christmastreejoe.com.
  2. Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom of the screen).
  3. Scroll down the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner to confirm.
🤖

Android Devices (Chrome)

Android Installation Guide

  1. Open Chrome and navigate to christmastreejoe.com.
  2. Tap the Three Dots menu icon in the top right corner.
  3. Select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Follow the on-screen prompt to complete installation.
