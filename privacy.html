Data Protection & Privacy Disclosures

Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 • Operated by Global Citizen Joe, LLC. in partnership with Xmas Made Inc.

1. Overview & Commitment

Global Citizen Joe, LLC. and Xmas Made Inc. ("Christmas Tree Joe", "we", "us", or "our") respect your privacy across christmastreejoe.com, christmastreejoe.store, and christmastreejoe.net[cite: 11]. We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade subscriber, driver, or grower data to third-party advertisers or data brokers[cite: 11].

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, password hashes, and user role metadata securely managed via Supabase Auth.
  • Delivery & Logistics Data: Residential/commercial addresses, gate codes, delivery windows, and January eco-pickup slots[cite: 11].
  • Billing & Payment Information: Tokenized transactions processed via Stripe (Level-1 PCI-DSS compliant)[cite: 11]. We do not store raw credit card numbers or CVV codes[cite: 11].
  • Carrier & Vendor Intake: Driver CDL credentials, insurance documents, and timber nursery lot harvest volumes submitted via Formspree[cite: 11].

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized to fulfill white-glove tree subscriptions, coordinate live driver GPS dispatch, send SMS arrival alerts, route pre-sold batch manifests to partner timber farms in Jefferson, NC, and manage circular recycling[cite: 11].

4. Third-Party Infrastructure & Security

Our platform utilizes trusted enterprise-grade infrastructure:

Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) PostgreSQL database isolation and JWT authentication.
Stripe PCI-compliant encrypted processing with 1% automated carbon removal allocation.
Formspree Encrypted intake of logistics dispatch manifests and partner bids.

5. Data Deletion & Privacy Inquiries

To request account deletion, data access exports, or privacy inquiries, contact our legal compliance team at: privacy@christmastreejoe.com or support@christmastreejoe.com[cite: 11].
