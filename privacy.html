Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 • Operated by Xmas Made Inc. (Global Citizen Joe, Inc.)

1. Information We Collect

Xmas Made Inc. ("Christmas Tree Joe", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. When you use our platform across christmastreejoe.com, christmastreejoe.store, christmastreejoe.net, or christmastreejoe.info, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and OAuth authentication tokens (Google, Microsoft, Facebook).
  • Fulfillment & Delivery Data: Residential/commercial addresses and delivery window preferences for white-glove setup.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely via encrypted gateways (Stripe, Square, PayPal Business). We do not store raw credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Vendor & Farm Metadata: Plantation location, GPS coordinates, species capacity, and wholesale contact details submitted on christmastreejoe.net.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized to facilitate white-glove tree subscriptions, coordinate post-holiday January eco-pickups, send delivery driver SMS arrival alerts, route pre-sold batch manifests to partner timber farms in Jefferson, NC, and manage regional market expansion.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Logistics Partners

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Customer addresses are shared exclusively with assigned delivery drivers and logistics dispatchers strictly for white-glove tree placement. Payments are handled via level-1 PCI-compliant processors.

4. Data Security & Storage

All web traffic is transmitted via 256-bit SSL encryption and protected by Cloudflare's edge firewall network. Account information is retained for active multi-year terms to maintain locked-in subscription pricing.

5. Contact Privacy Officer

To request account deletion, data access exports, or privacy inquiries, contact our legal team at: support@christmastreejoe.com.
