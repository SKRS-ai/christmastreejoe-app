Appalachian Classic

Fraser Fir

Dark green needles with silvery-green undersides. Rigid, upward-facing branches engineered by nature to support heavy heirloom glass ornaments.

Needle Retention: Superior (5/5)

Branch Rigidity: High Support

Aroma Profile: Rich Mountain Pine

Lush & Fragrant

Douglas Fir

Soft, light-green needles radiating in all directions. Famous for its exceptionally dense, pyramidal symmetry and sweet refreshing citrus aroma.

Needle Retention: Very Good (4/5)

Branch Rigidity: Medium Support

Aroma Profile: Sweet Citrus Pine

Architectural Elegance

Noble Fir

Short, stiff, upward-curving bluish-green needles. Features beautifully tiered horizontal branches designed for layered ornament styling.

Needle Retention: Superior (5/5)

Branch Rigidity: Maximum Support

Aroma Profile: Woodsy Mint & Pine
